Welcome

I am a PhD researcher in Environmental Engineering with an interdisciplinary background in Environmental Economics (MSc) and Philosophy, Politics, and Economics (BSc). My doctoral work centers around the role of energy/exergy in aggregate production functions in integrated assessmnet models. In my current project, I am adapting an IAM of the energy transition by including an energy-based production function, to understand implications for optimal climate policy.

Research Topics

  • Energy and technology in production frameworks
  • Social cost of carbon and optimal climate policy
  • Energy transition and mineral constraints
  • Ecologically Unequal Exchange

Models & Methods

  • Integrated Assessment Models (IAM)
  • Dynamic Stochastic General Equilibrium (DSGE)
  • Input-output
  • Causal inference and impact evaluation
